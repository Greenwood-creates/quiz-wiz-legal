Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 — Contact: isaiah.greenwood01@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains what Quiz Wizards ("the App", "we") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. The App generates AI-powered study quizzes from topics or documents you provide.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account info — a Firebase user ID and your sign-in identity. You can use the App anonymously, or sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In, which may share your name and email address depending on your provider settings.
  • Profile info you provide — display name.
  • Quiz content — topics you type in, quizzes generated for you, your quiz attempts and answers, and your study statistics, streaks, and mastery data.
  • Documents you upload — PDF and text files you choose to turn into quizzes. These are sent to our backend and processed by Google's Gemini model to generate quiz questions. Do not upload documents containing sensitive personal information.
  • Purchase data — Apple transaction receipts for subscriptions, which our servers verify with Apple to activate Pro. We never see your payment details.

2. How We Use It

  • To authenticate you and operate your account (including anonymous accounts).
  • To generate quizzes from your topics and documents using AI.
  • To save your quiz library, attempt history, stats, and streaks, and sync them to your account.
  • To verify your subscription purchases and enforce the free daily generation limit.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, serve third-party ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

3. Who Can See It

Your quizzes, documents, stats, and profile are private to your account — the App has no public content or social features, and nothing you create is visible to other users.

4. Storage & Retention

Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (United States). Deleting your account from Profile → Delete Account removes your profile, quizzes, attempts, and stats, and removes the sign-in record in Firebase Authentication. Uploaded documents are processed to generate quizzes and are not retained as separate files beyond the generated content.

5. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, database (Firestore), and server functions. See Firebase's privacy terms and Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Vertex AI (Gemini) — processes your topics and uploaded documents to generate quiz content. See Google Cloud's privacy notice.
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, and the App Store for subscription billing. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

6. Your Choices

  • Use the App anonymously without providing a name or email.
  • Edit your display name anytime in the app.
  • Delete your account — and all associated data — from Profile → Delete Account, or by emailing us at the address below.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

7. Security

Data is transmitted over TLS and access is restricted by Firestore security rules — sensitive account fields can only be written by our servers. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use industry-standard platform protections.

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date will change and material changes will be noted in the app.

10. Contact

Questions, deletion requests, or privacy concerns: isaiah.greenwood01@gmail.com.
