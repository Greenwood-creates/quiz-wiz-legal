Support

Need help with Quiz Wizards? The fastest answers are below. For anything else, email isaiah.greenwood01@gmail.com.

Subscriptions

Start a free trial or subscribe — tap the Pro banner in the app and choose a plan. New subscribers get 7 days free.

Cancel or change your plan — iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Quiz Wizards. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period.

Restore a purchase — open the Pro screen in the app and tap "Restore purchases". Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID used to subscribe.

Refunds — purchases are billed by Apple; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Account & Data

Sync across devices — sign in with Apple or Google from the Profile tab. Guest (anonymous) data stays on that device until you link a sign-in.

Delete your account and data — Profile → Delete Account removes your profile, quizzes, and stats permanently. You can also email us a deletion request.

Generating Quizzes

Free limit — free accounts get one quiz generation per day; Pro removes the limit.

Documents — PDF and TXT files are supported (up to 20 MB). Scanned PDFs work — the AI reads images too.

Generation fails? — check your connection and try again; failed generations never use your daily free quiz. If it persists, email us.
